Description

UX/UI design for the beruby cashback portal, as well as creation of digital marketing materials including banners, newsletters, and landing pages.

The project’s goal was to simplify the interface and improve users’ understanding of the cashback value proposition. Work focused on visual hierarchy, noise reduction, and clarity of the primary calls to action.

Key components were redesigned, the sign-up flow was optimized, and visual consistency across sections was improved.
