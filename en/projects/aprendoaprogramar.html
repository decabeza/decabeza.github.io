Description

Aprendo a Programar is an educational project that I founded and designed with the goal of bringing programming closer to children in a clear, visual and accessible way. I was responsible for product definition, learning experience design and visual identity, with a strong focus on interface simplicity and progressive understanding of concepts.

In addition to pedagogical and visual design, I handled video editing and voice-over, working on narrative, pacing and tone to maintain attention and support self-directed learning. All audiovisual material was conceived to reinforce logic, creativity and student confidence.

The project combines design, education and audiovisual content into a coherent ecosystem, aimed at helping children learn programming in an intuitive, practical and motivating way.
