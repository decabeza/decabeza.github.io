Description

I worked as a User Experience Designer on several digital projects for the Madrid City Council, focused on the modernization of public services and the promotion of open government during the term of Manuela Carmena.

The most relevant project was Decide Madrid, a citizen participation and direct democracy web platform designed to enable active citizen involvement in public decision-making. My work focused on designing clear, accessible and understandable flows for a wide range of users, reducing entry barriers and improving the overall participation experience.

I also contributed to the evolution of CONSUL Democracy, the open source core platform of the project, adopted by numerous cities and organizations at both national and international levels. This work helped consolidate a reusable, scalable platform aligned with principles of transparency, accessibility and usability in institutional contexts.
